Cantor · device import · I7 · the flow

This phone

Bringing music in is not a wizard. The phone is a home, the way a node is, and it gets the page a node gets: a clef, a stave of facts and one act in the coda. The first act on that page brings the music in, and every later visit (looking again, leaving a folder out, a file gone missing) happens on the same page, in the same Folio grammar. Nothing here is a new kind of control. It is the roster, the three inks, the working rule, the fermata and the strike, pointed at the phone.

WordsOne word per thing

"Import", "scan", "library" and "sync" never reach the screen. They are the code's words. A person has music on their phone, and Cantor brings it in.

1 · Where it startsThe phone is the first home in the roster

The nodes blind lists every place songs live. The phone joins it as the first entry, set apart from the nodes by a rest. Its mark is the phone's seal (Settings' clef), with the spindle at its centre where the seal had a dot: the same ring every imported song carries. That is how a song's mark and its home's mark say they belong together.

3 · What's thereThe summary, before anything comes in

Listing MediaStore is metadata only, under a second for hundreds of files. So the summary is drawn before any file is opened, and only what the person keeps is inspected. While the list is read, the act says Reading under the plain sine rule. The stave fills as the folders are found, each row landing when the spine reaches it, as in Arrival. Then the act becomes the count. Press REPLAY READING, then tap folders.

4 · Bringing inA measured rule, and the clef shows the record being read

Only the kept files are inspected and their art saved, so done / total is honest. That makes it F6's measured rule: straight from the left up to the fraction, still waving after it, straight at 100%, then retracting. The flourish is the clef. Each album's thumbnail is drawn through the cover lens the moment it is saved, and changes cell by cell to the next album, the way a terminal rewrites itself. The person watches their record collection go past. It is computed, never stored, and costs microseconds.

5 · ArrivalThe songs land album by album

The commit is one transaction, so the songs exist all at once. They are shown in order: when the blind lifts (or on See them), the camera goes to this week, and each album's marks open out of a point together, one album after another, the way a lens change opens a mark. The meta line says it once, 310 ARRIVED FROM THIS PHONE, then returns to the plain count the next time the field is opened. No toast. At L0 it is the same sentence: each album's cluster opens, cluster by cluster.

6 · AfterwardsThe phone's page, the way a node's is

Once read, This phone opens on what is here, the folders it reads, and anything that needs a word. The coda is Look again.

Looking again, like VLC

  • Every time Cantor opens, it asks MediaStore for its generation number. Same number: nothing to do, and no work at all. Higher: only rows changed since then are listed, and only new or changed files are inspected. This is the I3/I4 machinery, already built.
  • While Cantor is open, a ContentObserver on the audio table can trigger the same look a few seconds after the phone finishes copying files. That is optional; opening the app covers most cases.
  • New files in a kept folder come in silently. They arrive in the field like any new song. The person already said yes to that folder.
  • A new folder is asked about, never taken. The roster's line says 2 NEW FOLDERS in ink; the page puts them first (6B).
  • A file that vanished is held, not deleted: MISSING with the fermata. Its tags and playlists stay. It re-attaches by fingerprint if it comes back anywhere.
  • Look again exists for the person who just copied an album over USB and doesn't want to restart. It runs the same look, with the sine rule; if nothing changed, the act morphs to Nothing new and back.

  • Nothing new after Look again: the rule retracts and the act morphs to Nothing new for two seconds (6A).
  • The phone database can't open: the roster line says UNAVAILABLE with the fermata; the raw reason goes to Diagnostics (F9d). Node songs are untouched.
  • A file couldn't be read during bringing in: counted on its own row, COULDN'T READ · 3, and tried again on the next look. A stored song for it stands as it was.
  • Protected (DRM): counted, LEFT ALONE, never an error.
  • The permission is withdrawn later in Android settings: the songs stay in the field, faint, with the fermata on the roster line (NOT ALLOWED NOW), and come back by themselves when it's restored.

8 · UnderneathWhat the flow asks of the code

  • Split scan into list (MediaStore rows, grouped by folder, with the voice-note guess), and bring in (inspect, resolve, art, commit) over the kept folders only. ScanProgress already has inspecting {done, total}; add the album whose art was just saved, for the clef.
  • Folder exclusion goes in the existing device_excluded_folder table. A folder is new when it is neither kept nor excluded. No migration should be needed; if "kept" needs its own row, that is a new table, not a change.
  • The voice-note guess is a small pure function in device/: folder names (WhatsApp, Telegram, Recordings, Voice, Call) plus MediaStore's own IS_RECORDING where present. It only decides the starting ink.
  • On open: deviceLibrary.scan() after start() once the permission is held, so a generation match costs one native call.
  • The roster gets a phone entry fed by deviceLibrary.store; the phone mark is the settings seal with the spindle (marks/seal.ts + the lens's spindle ratio).
  • Arrival needs the field to know which entities are new in this commit, so it can open them album by album. That is one set of ids handed to the existing open-from-a-point clock.
